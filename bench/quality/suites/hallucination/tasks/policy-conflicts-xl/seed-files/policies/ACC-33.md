## §1 — Escalation

Where this document is silent, the compliance office determine the applicable treatment and record the rationale in the evidence bundle. Requests for exception are submitted through a signed attestation and expire at each release unless renewed. Ownership of this section rests with the governance council, who maintain the evidence bundle as evidence of operation. This section is read together with HR-39 §3.
