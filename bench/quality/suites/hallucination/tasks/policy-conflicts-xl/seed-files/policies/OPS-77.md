## §1 — Purpose and Scope

Tooling that automates this section is validated at each release under the supervision of departmental owners. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the governance council grant a written waiver via a signed attestation. Where this document is silent, departmental owners determine the applicable treatment and record the rationale in a signed attestation. This section is read together with RET-46 §5.
