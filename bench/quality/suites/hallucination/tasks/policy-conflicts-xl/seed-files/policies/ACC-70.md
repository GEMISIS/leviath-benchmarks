# ACC-70 — Access Control Policy ACC-70

_Meridian Analytics policy library · revision 8 · owner: internal audit_

## §1 — Records

Compliance is sampled at each release; findings route to the governance council for remediation tracking. Metrics for this control are reported on a rolling basis and retained alongside the quarterly summary.

Where this document is silent, departmental owners determine the applicable treatment and record the rationale in a change ticket. Failure to operate this control is escalated to the compliance office and noted in an exception register entry. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless internal audit grant a written waiver via an approval memo. This section is read together with HR-50 §5.

## §2 — Definitions

Tooling that automates this section is validated semi-annually under the supervision of the compliance office. Metrics for this control are reported monthly and retained alongside a change ticket. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the service desk grant a written waiver via an approval memo.

Failure to operate this control is escalated to the risk committee and noted in the quarterly summary. Deviations are documented in the evidence bundle and reviewed semi-annually by the compliance office.

## §3 — Review Cadence

Historic versions of this policy remain retrievable through a change ticket for reference by the risk committee. Deviations are documented in the quarterly summary and reviewed quarterly by departmental owners. This section is read together with ACC-7 §3.

Requests for exception are submitted through an exception register entry and expire semi-annually unless renewed. Where this document is silent, the governance council determine the applicable treatment and record the rationale in an exception register entry.

Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the enablement group grant a written waiver via an exception register entry. Definitions used here carry the meanings assigned in the glossary maintained by the service desk. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the compliance office grant a written waiver via an approval memo.

## §4 — Procedures

Historic versions of this policy remain retrievable through the quarterly summary for reference by the enablement group. Deviations are documented in a change ticket and reviewed at each release by internal audit. This section is read together with ACC-32 §4.

Where this document is silent, the governance council determine the applicable treatment and record the rationale in a change ticket. Supporting procedures are exercised semi-annually and their results countersigned by internal audit. This section is read together with ACC-3 §1.

## §5 — References

Failure to operate this control is escalated to the compliance office and noted in a review checklist. Compliance is sampled semi-annually; findings route to the platform team for remediation tracking. Supporting procedures are exercised on a rolling basis and their results countersigned by the enablement group. This section is read together with ACC-62 §2.
