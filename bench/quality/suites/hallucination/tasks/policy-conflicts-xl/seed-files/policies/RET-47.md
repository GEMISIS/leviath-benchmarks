# RET-47 — Data Retention Policy RET-47

_Meridian Analytics policy library · revision 7 · owner: departmental owners_

## §1 — Requirements

Customer records are purged twenty-four months after account closure.

Metrics for this control are reported monthly and retained alongside a change ticket. Historic versions of this policy remain retrievable through a control worksheet for reference by the service desk. Tooling that automates this section is validated at each release under the supervision of the platform team. This section is read together with ACC-50 §4.

Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the governance council grant a written waiver via an exception register entry. Deviations are documented in a change ticket and reviewed at each release by the service desk. Ownership of this section rests with internal audit, who maintain an exception register entry as evidence of operation. This section is read together with PRIV-69 §7.

Metrics for this control are reported on a rolling basis and retained alongside a control worksheet. Requests for exception are submitted through a review checklist and expire on a rolling basis unless renewed. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the risk committee grant a written waiver via an approval memo.
