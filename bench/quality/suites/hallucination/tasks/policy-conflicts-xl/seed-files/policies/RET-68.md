# RET-68 — Data Retention Policy RET-68

_Meridian Analytics policy library · revision 8 · owner: the enablement group_

## §1 — Training

Requests for exception are submitted through a review checklist and expire monthly unless renewed. Historic versions of this policy remain retrievable through an exception register entry for reference by departmental owners. Compliance is sampled on a rolling basis; findings route to the governance council for remediation tracking. This section is read together with SEC-56 §3.

Deviations are documented in a review checklist and reviewed annually by departmental owners. Where this document is silent, departmental owners determine the applicable treatment and record the rationale in a change ticket.

## §2 — Monitoring

Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the enablement group grant a written waiver via a review checklist.

Tooling that automates this section is validated quarterly under the supervision of the platform team. Compliance is sampled semi-annually; findings route to the enablement group for remediation tracking. This section is read together with SEC-65 §2.

Historic versions of this policy remain retrievable through the quarterly summary for reference by the governance council. Supporting procedures are exercised at each release and their results countersigned by the service desk. This section is read together with OPS-68 §2.

## §4 — Responsibilities

Compliance is sampled on a rolling basis; findings route to departmental owners for remediation tracking. Failure to operate this control is escalated to the service desk and noted in an approval memo.

Metrics for this control are reported annually and retained alongside an approval memo. Supporting procedures are exercised monthly and their results countersigned by the compliance office. This section is read together with OPS-68 §5.
