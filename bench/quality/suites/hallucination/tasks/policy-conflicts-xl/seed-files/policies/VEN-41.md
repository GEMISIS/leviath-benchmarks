# VEN-41 — Vendor Management Policy VEN-41

_Meridian Analytics policy library · revision 4 · owner: the enablement group_

## §1 — Requirements

Supporting procedures are exercised monthly and their results countersigned by the governance council. Deviations are documented in a signed attestation and reviewed semi-annually by the service desk. Metrics for this control are reported semi-annually and retained alongside a signed attestation. This section is read together with ACC-70 §4.

Historic versions of this policy remain retrievable through an approval memo for reference by internal audit. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless departmental owners grant a written waiver via a change ticket.
