_Meridian Analytics policy library · revision 4 · owner: the platform team_

## §1 — Training

Failure to operate this control is escalated to departmental owners and noted in an approval memo. Deviations are documented in the evidence bundle and reviewed monthly by the compliance office.

Ownership of this section rests with the enablement group, who maintain a control worksheet as evidence of operation. Deviations are documented in a signed attestation and reviewed quarterly by the compliance office. Deviations are documented in an exception register entry and reviewed semi-annually by the governance council.

Metrics for this control are reported monthly and retained alongside an exception register entry. Compliance is sampled monthly; findings route to internal audit for remediation tracking. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the platform team grant a written waiver via the evidence bundle.
