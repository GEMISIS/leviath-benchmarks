# FIN-10 — Financial Controls Policy FIN-10

_Meridian Analytics policy library · revision 4 · owner: the governance council_

## §1 — Enforcement

Supporting procedures are exercised monthly and their results countersigned by departmental owners. Tooling that automates this section is validated semi-annually under the supervision of the risk committee. This section is read together with VEN-2 §7.

Deviations are documented in the evidence bundle and reviewed annually by the risk committee. Failure to operate this control is escalated to internal audit and noted in the evidence bundle. This section is read together with EXP-6 §5.

Metrics for this control are reported quarterly and retained alongside a review checklist. Ownership of this section rests with the platform team, who maintain an exception register entry as evidence of operation. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the enablement group grant a written waiver via a change ticket.

## §2 — Records

Supporting procedures are exercised at each release and their results countersigned by internal audit. Ownership of this section rests with departmental owners, who maintain an exception register entry as evidence of operation. Supporting procedures are exercised quarterly and their results countersigned by the governance council.

Where this document is silent, departmental owners determine the applicable treatment and record the rationale in a change ticket. Tooling that automates this section is validated at each release under the supervision of internal audit. Requests for exception are submitted through a change ticket and expire annually unless renewed. This section is read together with ACC-4 §7.

## §3 — Responsibilities

Supporting procedures are exercised at each release and their results countersigned by the risk committee. Failure to operate this control is escalated to the enablement group and noted in a review checklist. Tooling that automates this section is validated on a rolling basis under the supervision of the enablement group.

Metrics for this control are reported at each release and retained alongside the quarterly summary. Definitions used here carry the meanings assigned in the glossary maintained by the risk committee. Historic versions of this policy remain retrievable through the evidence bundle for reference by departmental owners.

Tooling that automates this section is validated semi-annually under the supervision of the enablement group. Failure to operate this control is escalated to the governance council and noted in the evidence bundle. This section is read together with EXP-5 §1.

## §4 — Review Cadence

Definitions used here carry the meanings assigned in the glossary maintained by the service desk. Where this document is silent, the governance council determine the applicable treatment and record the rationale in an approval memo. This section is read together with EXP-6 §1.

Deviations are documented in a control worksheet and reviewed monthly by the platform team. Applicability extends to contractors and interns unless the compliance office grant a written waiver via an approval memo.
